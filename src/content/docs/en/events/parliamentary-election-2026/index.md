On 5 November 2026, the Republic Electoral Commission adopted its final report on the 25 October election and repeat voting at 53 polling stations on 1 November.[^1]

| Electoral list | Votes | Seats |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Student List — Students Win | 2,354,417 | 140 |
| Aleksandar Vučić — United Serbia | 1,731,323 | 103 |
| Alliance of Vojvodina Hungarians | 35,461 | 2 |
| Albanian Coalition | 27,683 | 2 |
| Rasim Ljajić — Ljudski | 20,638 | 1 |
| Usame Zukorlić (SPP) | 12,440 | 1 |
| SDA Sandžak | 12,596 | 1 |

The table includes electoral lists that won seats; the source records all others. Seats belong to lists, not to individual coalition parties.
