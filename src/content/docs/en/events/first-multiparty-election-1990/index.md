Following the return of multiparty politics to Serbia, parliamentary elections were held on 9 and 23 December 1990. The National Assembly's official history reports 194 seats for the Socialist Party of Serbia, 19 for the Serbian Renewal Movement and 7 for the Democratic Party. Slobodan Milošević was elected president at the same time.[^1]

This is the documented start of the survey period, **before the SFRY had completed its dissolution**. The election results alone establish neither a party's later political activity nor its present legal status.
