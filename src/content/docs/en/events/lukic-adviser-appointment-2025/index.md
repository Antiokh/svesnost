On **16 August 2025**, Serbia's Ministry of Information and Telecommunications appointed Aleksandar Lukić a special adviser to the minister. The ministry provided a copy of its decision to Transparency Serbia following an access-to-information request.[^1]

The responsibilities described include expert advice, coordination within the minister's office, contact with other state institutions and protocol arrangements. The document does not establish how long the appointment lasted.[^1]

[^1]: **Document · appointment confirmed** — [Ministry decision no. 003491105 2025 13460 001 006 000 001, dated 16 August 2025](https://www.transparentnost.org.rs/images/Odgovor_-_Ministarstvo_IT_-_savetnici.pdf), attached to a reply to Transparency Serbia. It does not specify an end date.
