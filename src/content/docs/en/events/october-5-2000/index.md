After the Federal Republic of Yugoslavia's presidential election on 24 September 2000, the Democratic Opposition of Serbia called for nationwide protests. The mass demonstration in Belgrade on 5 October accompanied demands that the election results be recognised. RTS identifies Vojislav Koštunica as the DOS presidential candidate and Slobodan Milošević as the incumbent and rival candidate.[^1]

**DOS was an electoral coalition**, not a single registered party. Its constituents did not necessarily share every policy or play identical roles in the demonstrations.
