In September 2008, a group of MPs separated from the Serbian Radical Party's parliamentary structure. On **10 October**, RTS reported the Serbian Progressive Party's registration, with Tomislav Nikolić and Aleksandar Vučić filing the documents. On **21 October**, its founding assembly elected Nikolić president and Vučić deputy leader.[^1]

The SNS and SRS are **separate parties**. The word 'split' describes political origins but does not imply automatic legal succession.
