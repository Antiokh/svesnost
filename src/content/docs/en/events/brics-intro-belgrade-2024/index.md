According to the *Bratstvo* publication dated **14 October 2024**, the **Introduction to BRICS+** meeting took place on **7 October** at the Russian House in Belgrade. It identified the Moscow–Belgrade International Eurasian Movement as organizer and included a speech attributed to Aleksandar Lukić.[^1]

Participation in an event does not itself establish membership in its organizing movement.

[^1]: **Publication · limited to the published text** — [*Bratstvo*, speeches from “Uvod u BRIKS+”](https://srbratstvo.rs/dugin-srbija-je-rusija-rusija-je-srbija-sva-izlaganja-na-skupu-uvod-u-briks-u-ruskom-domu/), published 14 October 2024; section “Др Александар Лукић — Историја и будућност БРИКС+”; event held 7 October 2024.
