According to the Historical Archives of Belgrade, the People's Radical Party was established on 20 January 1881, the Progressive Party two days later, and the Liberal Party's programme appeared on 13 October 1881.[^1]

The event date marks the beginning of the process; the three parties were not all founded on the same day.
