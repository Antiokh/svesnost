[Scanned decision attached to the ministry's reply to Transparency Serbia (PDF)](https://www.transparentnost.org.rs/images/Odgovor_-_Ministarstvo_IT_-_savetnici.pdf).

A decision dated **16 August 2025** appoints Aleksandar Lukić, a doctor of philosophy from Belgrade, special adviser to the Minister of Information and Telecommunications of Serbia. It lists expert, coordinating and protocol duties within the minister's office.

The document establishes the appointment as of that date, but does not by itself establish whether the role continued through November 2026.
