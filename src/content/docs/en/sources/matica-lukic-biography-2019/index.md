The organizers state that Lukić was born in Belgrade in 1970, received his doctorate in philosophy at the Faculty of Philosophy, published *Obrnuti Vavilon*, and worked on philosophy events, radio programming and photography.

The lecture was scheduled for **10 July 2019**; the webpage's original publication date is not explicitly stated. An organizer's biographical note is not an independent verification of every detail.
