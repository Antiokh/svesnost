[Historical Archives of Belgrade: party history](https://stalnapostavka.arhiv-beograda.org/en/chapters/political-life/development-of-party-life.html).

The exhibition traces parties established from 1881, their leaders and programmes and political life between the world wars. It is an archival historical publication rather than an original founding-minute document.
