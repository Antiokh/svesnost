[Panagiotis Karakinaris, *The Political Status in Serbia after the War of 1999*, pp. 9–10](https://dspace.lib.uom.gr/bitstream/2159/13872/1/Karakinaris_Msc2009.pdf).

Although DOS is often called a coalition of **18 parties**, the enumerated membership includes a regional coalition and a trade-union association. **Eighteen constituent organisations** is the more precise description. The DA–ND–DC subgroup had previously formed the DAN alliance; its three members are listed separately, not as a nineteenth constituent. This records the position **in 2000**, not current party-registration status. [Spyridon Kotsovilis, *Six Degrees of Revolution*, p. 118](https://www.collectionscanada.gc.ca/obj/thesescanada/vol2/QMM/TC-QMM-114184.pdf) also enumerates the eighteen initial components.
