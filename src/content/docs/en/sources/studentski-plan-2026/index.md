[Official Studentski plan (PDF, Serbian)](https://gg.studenti.org.rs/studentski-plan.pdf) · [HTML edition](https://gg.studenti.org.rs/lat/program/studentski-plan/).

The **eleven-point plan**, grouped under three headings, promises measures including lustration, a special prosecution police, free textbooks, indefinite employment contracts after twelve months, preferential **rental and purchase** of housing, and improved access to rural healthcare. Its wording is sometimes broader than the [detailed programme](/en/sources/studentska-lista-program-2026/). See the [English review](/en/materials/studentska-lista-program-2026/) for a comparison of the two documents.
