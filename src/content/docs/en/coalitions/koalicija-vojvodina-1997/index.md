**Vojvodina Coalition** — regional coalition. A 2009 academic survey of DOS identifies this organisation and its associated representative among DOS's components in 2000. This does not establish its current legal registration or a continuing leadership role.[^1]

## Associated leader or representative in 2000

- [Dragan Veselinov](/en/persons/dragan-veselinov/) — Historical leader or representative of a DOS component around 2000.[^1]
