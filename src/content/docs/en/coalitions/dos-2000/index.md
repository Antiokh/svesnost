**Democratic Opposition of Serbia (DOS)**. The Democratic Opposition of Serbia (DOS) was **a coalition of political parties**, not a single registered party. In 2000 it contested Slobodan Milošević's government. Its presidential candidate for the 24 September 2000 FRY election was **Vojislav Koštunica**. The coalition included the Democratic Party led by **Zoran Đinđić**, among other organisations.[^1][^2]
