The **Democratic Opposition of Serbia (DOS)** formed in January 2000 as an alliance of diverse organisations opposing the government of Slobodan Milošević. Its candidate in the September 2000 federal presidential election was Vojislav Koštunica. Its constituents remained distinct organisations following the 5 October political transition.[^1]

## The 18 components of DOS in 2000

1. [Democratic Party](/en/parties/demokratska-stranka/) — political party; [Zoran Đinđić](/en/persons/zoran-djindjic/).[^1]
2. [Democratic Party of Serbia (DSS, 2000)](/en/parties/nova-demokratska-stranka-srbije/) — political party; [Vojislav Koštunica](/en/persons/vojislav-kostunica/).[^1]
3. [Civic Alliance of Serbia](/en/parties/gradjanski-savez-srbije/) — political party; [Goran Svilanović](/en/persons/goran-svilanovic/).[^1]
4. [Christian Democratic Party of Serbia](/en/parties/demohriscanska-stranka-srbije/) — political party; [Vladan Batić](/en/persons/vladan-batic/).[^1]
5. [New Serbia](/en/parties/nova-srbija/) — political party; [Velimir Ilić](/en/persons/velimir-ilic/).[^1]
6. [League of Social Democrats of Vojvodina](/en/parties/liga-socijaldemokrata-vojvodine/) — political party; [Nenad Čanak](/en/persons/nenad-canak/).[^1]
7. [Social Democratic Union](/en/parties/socijaldemokratska-unija/) — political party; [Žarko Korać](/en/persons/zarko-korac/).[^1]
8. [Alliance of Vojvodina Hungarians](/en/parties/savez-vojvodjanskih-madjara/) — political party; [József Kasza](/en/persons/jozef-kasa/).[^1]
9. [Reformist Democratic Party of Vojvodina (RDSV)](/en/parties/reformisti-vojvodine-2000/) — political party; [Miodrag Mile Isakov](/en/persons/mile-isakov/).[^1]
10. [Vojvodina Coalition](/en/coalitions/koalicija-vojvodina-1997/) — regional coalition; [Dragan Veselinov](/en/persons/dragan-veselinov/).[^1]
11. [Social Democracy](/en/parties/socijaldemokratija-2000/) — political party; [Vuk Obradović](/en/persons/vuk-obradovic/).[^1]
12. [Movement for Democratic Serbia](/en/parties/pokret-za-demokratsku-srbiju/) — political party; [Momčilo Perišić](/en/persons/momcilo-perisic/).[^1]
13. [Sandžak Democratic Party](/en/parties/sandzacka-demokratska-partija/) — political party; [Rasim Ljajić](/en/persons/rasim-ljajic/).[^1]
14. [League for Šumadija](/en/parties/liga-za-sumadiju-1997/) — political party; [Branislav Kovačević](/en/persons/branislav-kovacevic/).[^1]
15. [Association of Free and Independent Trade Unions (ASNS)](/en/institutions/asns/) — trade-union association; [Dragan Milovanović](/en/persons/dragan-milovanovic/).[^1]
16. [Democratic Alternative](/en/parties/demokratska-alternativa/) — political party; [Nebojša Čović](/en/persons/nebojsa-covic/).[^1]
17. [New Democracy](/en/parties/nova-demokratija-1990/) — political party; [Dušan Mihajlović](/en/persons/dusan-mihajlovic/).[^1]
18. [Democratic Centre](/en/parties/demokratski-centar-1996/) — political party; [Dragoljub Mićunović](/en/persons/dragoljub-micunovic/).[^1]

Although DOS is often called a coalition of **18 parties**, the enumerated membership includes a regional coalition and a trade-union association. **Eighteen constituent organisations** is the more precise description. The DA–ND–DC subgroup had previously formed the DAN alliance; its three members are listed separately, not as a nineteenth constituent. This records the position **in 2000**, not current party-registration status.[^1][^2]
