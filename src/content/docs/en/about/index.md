Svesnost is an independent politics and society encyclopedia built with Astro and Starlight.

Its original edition is in Serbian Cyrillic; Russian and English are separately edited optional editions with independent navigation and search.

Read our [editorial policy](/en/editorial-policy/) or suggest corrections on [GitHub](https://github.com/Antiokh/svesnost). Editorial governance will be specified separately.
