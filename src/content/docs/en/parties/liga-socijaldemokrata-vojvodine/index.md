**League of Social Democrats of Vojvodina** — political party. A source-based introductory profile for the 1990–2026 period. Complete leadership histories, party programmes and membership records have not been established, and this entry remains open for further documented research.

## Official party-register record

Foundation date recorded in the register: **1990-07-14**.[^1]

The foundation date of a registered legal entity may differ from when it adopted its present name. Registration does not prove participation in a given election, and a legal representative is not necessarily the party president.

## Leaders and significant figures

- [Bojan Kostreš](/en/persons/bojan-kostres/) — Person recorded as the **legal representative** in the official register (31.10.2026).[^2]
- [Nenad Čanak](/en/persons/nenad-canak/) — Founder or co-founder.[^3]
