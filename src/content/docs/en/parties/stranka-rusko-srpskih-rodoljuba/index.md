**Party of Russian–Serbian Patriots** — registered as a Russian national-minority party. Registration as a Russian national-minority party **does not establish** that the organisation represents the majority of Russians in Serbia, that its members have Russian ethnicity, or that it is supported by the Russian state.[^1]

## Official political-party register extract

- Foundation date: **2025-11-25**
- Registration decision date: **2026-03-02**
- Legal representative in the register: [Dejan Banković](/en/persons/dejan-bankovic/)[^1]

## Programmes, elections and public statements

No reliable standalone programme has been reviewed for this party. Its ideology must not be inferred from its name.
