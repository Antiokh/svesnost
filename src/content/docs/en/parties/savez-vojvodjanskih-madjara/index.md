**Alliance of Vojvodina Hungarians** (VMSZ) — political party. An initial source-based party profile. This is **not** a complete history or an independent verification of current registration or leadership.

## Documented electoral activity in 2026

The election commission certified an Alliance of Vojvodina Hungarians list headed by Bálint Pásztor.[^1]

Votes and seats belong to an **electoral list**, not automatically to every party participating in it. The commission's 5 November final report records votes and seats for entire electoral lists.[^2]
