**Yugoslav Radical Union** — historical political organisation. A political organisation prominent after the Kingdom of Yugoslavia's 1935 election, led by Milan Stojadinović. It was distinct from the People's Radical Party founded in 1881.[^1]

Historical organisation, not automatically identical to contemporary parties with similar names.
