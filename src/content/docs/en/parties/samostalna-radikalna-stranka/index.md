**Independent Radical Party** — historical political organisation. Formed from the younger Radical faction after disputes over the Obrenović dynasty. Its programme appeared in Odjek in 1904; leaders included Ljuba Živković, Ljuba Stojanović and Ljuba Davidović.[^1]

Historical organisation, not automatically identical to contemporary parties with similar names.
