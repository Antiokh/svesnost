**Serbian Renewal Movement** — political party. A source-based introductory profile for the 1990–2026 period. Complete leadership histories, party programmes and membership records have not been established, and this entry remains open for further documented research.

## Official party-register record

Foundation date recorded in the register: **1990-03-14**.[^1]

The foundation date of a registered legal entity may differ from when it adopted its present name. Registration does not prove participation in a given election, and a legal representative is not necessarily the party president.
