**Communist Party of Yugoslavia** — historical political organisation. Founded in 1919. Yugoslav communists received nearly 200,000 votes in the 1920 election, and the party was banned in 1921.[^1]

Historical organisation, not automatically identical to contemporary parties with similar names.
