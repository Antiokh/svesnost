**Progressive Party (1881)** — historical political organisation. Founded on 22 January 1881 by a circle of moderate conservatives. Its leaders included Milan Piroćanac, Milutin Garašanin and Stojan Novaković.[^1]

Historical organisation, not automatically identical to contemporary parties with similar names.
