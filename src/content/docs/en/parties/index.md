## Sources and method

Inclusion does not establish that every organisation retained legal registration in November 2026 or participated in the 2026 election. Electoral lists can be coalitions or citizens' groups rather than registered political parties.

The “Student List — Students Win” was an **electoral list submitted by a citizens' group**, not automatically a registered political party. Its [programme](/en/materials/studentska-lista-program-2026/) is documented separately.

The official open-data portal offers a dated CSV/XLSX snapshot of party registrations and removals (23 April 2026). This is a curated selection, not a substitute for examining the complete file.

- [Live official political-party register](https://upit.rps.gov.rs/)
- [Open dataset with registration and deletion dates](https://data.gov.rs/sr/datasets/politichke-stranke/)
- [Electoral Commission: 2026 electoral lists](https://www.rik.parlament.gov.rs/vest/sr/718695/50-sednica-republicke-izborne-komisije)
