**Party of Freedom and Justice** — political party. A source-based introductory profile for the 1990–2026 period. Complete leadership histories, party programmes and membership records have not been established, and this entry remains open for further documented research.

## Official party-register record

Foundation date recorded in the register: **2008-02-05**.[^1]

The foundation date of a registered legal entity may differ from when it adopted its present name. Registration does not prove participation in a given election, and a legal representative is not necessarily the party president.

## Leaders and significant figures

- [Dragan Đilas](/en/persons/dragan-djilas/) — Person recorded as the **legal representative** in the official register (31.10.2026).[^2]

The register's 2008 foundation date should not be described as the date when this party adopted its present name. The renaming and later political organisation need separate verification.

## 2026 election

The party was expressly listed within one of the certified electoral lists for Serbia's 25 October 2026 parliamentary election. Votes and seats were awarded to electoral lists, not allocated separately to each party in a coalition.[^3]
