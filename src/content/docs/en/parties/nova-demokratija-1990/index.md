**New Democracy (Serbia)** — historical political party. New Democracy was a historical Serbian political party from the early multiparty period. A list of registrations published in the Serbian Official Gazette, issue 13/2000, records its registration on **7 August 1990**, naming **Dušan Mihajlović** as chairman.[^1]

According to the National Assembly's historical account, the joint **SPS–JUL–New Democracy** electoral list won **110 seats** in the 1997 parliamentary election. These were the list's seats, not New Democracy's separate total.[^2]

## Leadership

- [Dušan Mihajlović](/en/persons/dusan-mihajlovic/) — chairman in the published historical register.[^1]
