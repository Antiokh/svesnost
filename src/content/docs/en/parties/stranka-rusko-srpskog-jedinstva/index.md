**Party of Russian–Serbian Unity** — registered as a Russian national-minority party. Registration as a Russian national-minority party **does not establish** that the organisation represents the majority of Russians in Serbia, that its members have Russian ethnicity, or that it is supported by the Russian state.[^1]

## Official political-party register extract

- Foundation date: **2026-08-09**
- Registration decision date: **2026-09-04**
- Legal representative in the register: [Rade Savić](/en/persons/rade-savic/)[^1]

## Programmes, elections and public statements

en

In campaign statements reported by Euronews Serbia in 2026, the party advocated ending EU accession talks and the existing Brussels Kosovo dialogue, involving Russia and China in talks, pursuing BRICS and an alliance of Orthodox countries, banning foreign-funded NGOs, increasing trade with Russia, educating young people in Russia and protecting workers. These are **the party's proposals**, not independently established policy outcomes.[^2]

Rade Savić was described as party leader and electoral-list submitter. RFE/RL also named four members, without establishing that they were party executives.[^3]

The “Party of Russian–Serbian Unity — Russians and Serbs Brothers Forever” list received **4,286 votes and no seats** in the final 2026 election results.[^4]
