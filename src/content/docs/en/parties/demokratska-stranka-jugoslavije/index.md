**Democratic Party of the Kingdom of Serbs, Croats and Slovenes** — historical political organisation. The Democratic Party became an important political organisation in the new Kingdom of Serbs, Croats and Slovenes. Ljubomir Davidović was among its founders.[^1]

Historical organisation, not automatically identical to contemporary parties with similar names.
