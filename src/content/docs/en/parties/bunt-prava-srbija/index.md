**BUNT — Real Serbia** — registered as a Russian national-minority party. Registration as a Russian national-minority party **does not establish** that the organisation represents the majority of Russians in Serbia, that its members have Russian ethnicity, or that it is supported by the Russian state.[^1]

## Official political-party register extract

- Foundation date: **2015-10-24**
- Registration decision date: **2015-12-17**
- Legal representative in the register: [Dušan Marković](/en/persons/dusan-markovic-bunt/)[^1]

## Programmes, elections and public statements

No reliable standalone programme has been reviewed for this party. Its ideology must not be inferred from its name.
