The **Serbian People's Party (SNP)** is not registered as a Russian national-minority party. The state register records it as an ordinary party founded on 4 December 2014 (registration decision 20 January 2015). It lists **Miljan Stojanović** as legal representative, while the party's website identifies **Nenad Popović** as founder and president. These are different roles.[^1][^2]

- [Nenad Popović](/en/persons/nenad-popovic/) — founder and president according to the party.[^2]
- [Miljan Stojanović](/en/persons/miljan-stojanovic/) — registered legal representative.[^1]

The party website says Popović headed a parliamentary friendship group with Russia and conducted business there. These are party-published claims, not an independent audit of those links.[^2]
