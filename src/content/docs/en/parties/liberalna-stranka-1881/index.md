**Liberal Party (1881)** — historical political organisation. Its programme was published on 13 October 1881 in the newspaper Srpska nezavisnost. Its leaders included Jovan Ristić, Jovan Avakumović and Stojan Ribarac.[^1]

Historical organisation, not automatically identical to contemporary parties with similar names.
