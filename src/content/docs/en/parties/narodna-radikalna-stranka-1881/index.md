**People's Radical Party (1881)** — historical political organisation. Founded on 20 January 1881; Nikola Pašić was among its leading figures. The party shaped Serbian political life across the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries.[^1]

Historical organisation, not automatically identical to contemporary parties with similar names.
