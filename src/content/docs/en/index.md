## About this encyclopedia

Svesnost is an independent encyclopedia of politics and society. Serbian Cyrillic is the primary edition. Russian and English are separate optional editorial editions, not automatic fallbacks.

Our evidence framework distinguishes documents, publications, testimony and rumors. Significant contested claims are accompanied by checkable sources and an explanation of what the evidence supports.

Read the [editorial policy](/en/editorial-policy/) or explore the [articles section](/en/materials/).
